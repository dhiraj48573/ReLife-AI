Who We Are

ReLife is a professional support platform dedicated to helping individuals navigate the complex journey of divorce and life transitions. We are a team of experienced counselors, legal experts, and support specialists committed to providing compassionate guidance during life's most challenging moments.

Founded on the principle that everyone deserves access to quality support during difficult times, ReLife has become a trusted resource for thousands of individuals seeking to rebuild their lives with confidence and dignity.

We believe that with the right support system, anyone can transform their challenges into opportunities for growth and new beginnings.

Our Story

How ReLife began and the journey that brought us here

2021

The Beginning

ReLife was born from Dhiraj Kumar experience as a family therapist. She saw countless individuals struggling to find affordable, accessible support during divorce and life transitions. The idea was simple: create a platform that combines professional expertise with the power of community support.

2023

Building the Platform

Joined by technology , the team began developing a secure, user-friendly platform that could connect people with the support they need. The focus was on creating a safe, confidential space where individuals could find both professional guidance and peer support.

2025

Growing Impact

With Niraj Kumar leading counseling services, ReLife expanded to serve thousands of users across the country. We developed comprehensive support programs, partnered with legal experts, and built a community of trained moderators dedicated to helping others.

Today

Making a Difference

Today, ReLife is a trusted platform that has helped over 10,000 individuals navigate their life transitions. We continue to innovate and expand our services, always staying true to our mission of providing compassionate, professional support to those who need it most.
